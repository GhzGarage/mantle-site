How publishing works Live

Tools → Mantle has two commands: Save to Mantle ("Upload this level as a private draft. Nobody else can see it.") and Publish. Both ask you to sign in first if you haven't. Publish also asks "What changed in this version?" for optional revision notes (up to 1,000 characters), which are kept with the revision.

Both run in the background, so you can keep working. The Mantle Publish tab shows each step, with a progress bar, a "Needs your attention" list and a Details log:

  1. Check this place

    Runs the same checks as "Check this place". If anything fails, you'll see "This place has N problem(s) to fix first."

  2. Prepare meshes and materials

    Converts the meshes and materials from your project that the level uses. A level built only from library assets skips this.

  3. Upload to Mantle

    Uploads the converted files and every script in the level's Scripts folder. Files you've uploaded before aren't sent again.

  4. Mantle processes and screens them

    Mantle processes the files and screens textures and scripts. Something that needs a moderator shows as "waiting for a Mantle moderator to look at it."

  5. Publish

    The first time you publish a level, this creates a new world with the level's name. Later publishes update that same world. Mantle saves your draft and, for Publish, makes it live as a new, numbered revision.

When it works, you'll see "Saved to Mantle. Nobody can see it until you publish." or "Published revision N. Anyone can join it now." If it stops, the tab says why and what to do. Cancel stops at any point without publishing anything, and Publish again carries on from where it stopped: everything already uploaded stays on Mantle.

For automation, the Mantle.Publish console command runs the same Publish on the open level and waits until it finishes (30 minutes at most by default, or set -TimeoutMinutes=N).

Revisions

  • Live Every publish creates a new revision, numbered in order. Until you publish, each save replaces your draft.
  • Live Running games keep going. Publishing doesn't disrupt players mid-game: games already running stay on the revision they started with. New games start on your latest revision.
  • In progress Previews and staged rollouts: try a new revision privately, then release it to a percentage of players before everyone else. Controls are coming to the editor.
  • In progress Rollback: go back to an earlier revision. Controls are coming to the editor. A revision that stops being live can be made live again for 180 days. A preview or staged revision that never went live can be for 30 days. After that you'll see "That revision is past its retention window and can no longer be made live."

How worlds get discovered Live

  • Catalogue and search: published worlds are listed newest first. Search covers world names, descriptions and creator names, and results can be sorted by Best match (the default), Most played or Newest. Your creator page lists all your worlds.
  • Featured: up to 12 worlds hand-picked by Mantle.
  • Charts: "Top 10 this week" shows the most played worlds of the past week. "New and rising" shows worlds that are picking up players, then the newest worlds.
  • Stats: your profile shows how many worlds you've published. World cards show how many people are playing now. Charts show unique players and play time. Planned Detailed stats for your own worlds.
