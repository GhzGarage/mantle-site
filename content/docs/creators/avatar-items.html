In progress The Mantle Cosmetics tab in the editor publishes avatar items to the Mantle catalogue with one button. You pick what you're making, the asset and a name. The plugin checks it against Mantle's rules, exports it, makes a thumbnail, uploads everything and follows the item through screening. Players find published items on the Avatar screen, which is still in progress.

What you can make

The panel's first drop-down picks the kind of item, and the second picks its slot. Each kind has its own page with the rules its asset has to meet.

The kind starts on Clothing, and the slot starts on the first one in its list. Everything in the catalogue is free. The panel has no price field, and paid items come later with the marketplace.

Publishing an item

  1. Sign in

    Uploading needs a creator token. See Signing in to publish. The top of the panel shows "Signed in as [your name]", or "Not signed in: Upload asks for a creator token (Tools > Mantle > Sign in with creator token)." If you aren't signed in, Upload to Mantle opens the sign-in window with "Sign in to publish this item to Mantle." once your checks pass. Check only and Preview on your own body need no sign-in.

  2. Open the panel

    Choose Tools → Mantle → Cosmetics.... The Mantle Cosmetics tab opens, headed "Publish to the Mantle catalog".

  3. Pick the kind and slot

    Under "What are you publishing?", pick the kind and then the slot. A line under them describes what that kind takes.

  4. Pick the asset

    Under "Asset", choose it from your project. The picker only offers the right type of asset for the kind. For an avatar, use Convert a character instead: see Publishing an avatar.

  5. Fill in the kind's details

    Some kinds show extra sections. See The panel's fields.

  6. Name it

    Under "Listing", give it a Name (players see it in the catalogue; choosing the asset fills it in with the asset's name if it's empty) and an optional Description.

  7. Choose who can use it

    Under "Who can use it", leave it on Public and tick the rights box, or choose Private. See Public and private items.

  8. Check it

    Check only runs every check and the export without uploading anything. Fix anything listed as a "Problem".

  9. Upload it

    Upload to Mantle checks again, then exports, uploads and publishes. It won't start while any check fails. See After you upload.

Socket offsets and the accessory's attachment use the same ranges: location up to 50 cm from the socket on each axis, rotation from -180 to 180 degrees (pitch, yaw, roll) and scale from 0.5 to 2. The attachment boxes stop at those values. Socket offsets let you type past them, and the check then refuses the value instead of clamping it.

You don't make the thumbnail yourself. The panel renders a 512 x 512 picture of the item every time it checks:

  • Avatar: a headshot with its face.
  • Clothing: on the body it was made on, framed on the clothing, with only the sections the item takes.
  • Accessory: on its own, in a three-quarter view from above.
  • Hair and emotes: the asset's own Unreal thumbnail. For a groom, the editor's thumbnail is an empty frame, so it uses the first group's cards mesh.
  • Overlay: the mask, drawn light on dark.

If nothing can be rendered, the thumbnail is a plain dark square. Files and details, at the bottom of the panel, lists each file the item uploads with its size, and says how the thumbnail was made. Once an upload starts, it lists each file's progress instead.

Previewing

Under Preview, choose Preview to see the item without uploading anything.

  • Clothing and hair: fitted by the same code players' games run. Under "Preview on", pick "The body it was made on" or "Another avatar by id" (itm_u followed by 32 hex digits). Tick "Play an animation (else the reference pose)" to play an animation, or leave it unticked to hold the reference pose.
  • Accessories: shown on a local MetaHuman body you choose under Authored on, or on a catalogue avatar. Attachment edits update the preview and are saved for that mesh and slot.
  • Avatars: posed the way players' games pose them, copying the UEFN mannequin's standing idle. The Mantle plugin turns IK Rig on itself and has its own UEFN mannequin, so there's nothing to add. If your project has a UEFN mannequin, the plugin uses it instead: it finds any skeletal mesh on the SK_UEFN_Mannequin skeleton and prefers SKM_UEFN_Mannequin (from the Game Animation Sample). With the plugin's own mannequin there's no idle to play, so the avatar holds the A-pose.
  • Overlays: drawn on a converted MetaHuman body or face with the same skin material players' clients use. Pick the body and face under Overlay images, or leave them empty to use Mantle's reference ones when your project has built them. The skin material is project content (/Game/Avatars/Appearance/Shared): without it the preview shows the mesh as it is, and the text next to Preview says the mask isn't drawn.

Previewing on a catalogue avatar needs you to be signed in. Clothing and hair say "Sign in to preview on avatars from the Mantle catalog." and accessories say "Sign in to preview accessories on catalog avatars." Avatar previews need no UEFN mannequin in your project. To see the avatar idle, add SKM_UEFN_Mannequin and its idle from the Game Animation Sample.

Public and private items In progress

Under "Who can use it" you choose one of two options:

  • Public: "Public: listed in the catalog for every player". This is the default. You also have to tick "I own this content or its licence permits public redistribution on Mantle". Without the tick you'll see "Tick the declaration to publish publicly, or choose Private." and Upload to Mantle stays greyed out. Choosing a new asset clears the tick.
  • Private: "Private: only you can use it". It needs no declaration.

Mantle also checks public items for copies. If another account uploaded the same content (or a re-exported copy of the same shape) first, you'll see "Another account uploaded this content first. Publish it privately, or contact support if it is yours." After three copyright strikes, public publishing is turned off for the account: "Public publishing is turned off for this account after repeated copyright strikes. You can still publish privately."

What the panel checks

Each check result is a line starting "Problem:" (it must be fixed), "Note:" (worth knowing, but it doesn't stop an upload) or "Done:" (something the plugin handled for you, such as uploading a lower LOD). When nothing is listed, you'll see "Every check passed." The line above the list says "Fix the problems below, then choose Upload to Mantle." or "Ready: choose Upload to Mantle."

After you upload

The upload runs in the background with a progress bar, so you can keep working. The status line shows each stage:

  1. Checking which files Mantle already has...

    Files your account has already uploaded are skipped, so nothing is sent twice. The log says, for example, "Mantle already has 3 of 5 files; 2 to upload".

  2. Uploading and processing files: N of M ready...

    Mantle checks each file and screens it. In Files and details, each file has a line such as "[file]: processing - Being screened". The first word is waiting, processing, ready or failed, and the rest says what's happening: "Waiting", "Uploading ([size])", "Being processed", "Being screened", "Waiting for a moderator", "Approved", "Already on Mantle" or "Retrying". A garment waits for the body it was made on to be approved first ("Waiting for its body shape", or "Waiting for [file] to be approved"). When Mantle is busy with other uploads, you'll see "Mantle is busy with other uploads; waiting for a turn". That isn't a failure.

  3. Creating the item...

    Once every file is approved, the plugin creates the item from them.

  4. Screening the item...

    Mantle screens the item's thumbnail, name and description. For a scar or tattoo it screens the mask and the normal map too. If something is flagged, a moderator looks at it. Textures were screened when each file was uploaded. Meshes pass a check when they are processed and are otherwise moderated through reports.

When it finishes, the status line says one of:

  • "Published [item id]. Players can find it in the catalog now."
  • "Published [item id]. A moderator will look at it; it appears in the catalog once approved."
  • "Published [item id]. Mantle is still screening it; it appears in the catalog once it passes." (if screening takes more than 5 minutes)

An item that hangs below the knee (footwear aside) is published with an extra line: "It hangs below the knee, so Mantle marks it "may clip in motion": the legs can show through it when the wearer moves."

Once approved, a public item is listed in the catalogue for everyone, and players can find more of your public items with "More by [creator]". A private item shows only for you. If the panel stops following an item before it's decided, look for it on the Avatar screen: it shows up there once it passes.

If a request fails because of a bad connection or a busy server, the plugin tries again on its own, up to five times, waiting 2, 5, 10, 20 and 30 seconds. If Mantle answers that you're going too fast, it waits a minute each time. If Mantle is busy with other uploads, a file waits for its turn for up to 10 minutes in all, and that doesn't use up a try. A file that is still processing after 15 minutes stops the upload with a message.

Cancel stops an upload at any time, and closing the tab does the same. Everything already uploaded stays on Mantle: "Cancelled. Upload again to continue where it stopped."

Mantle checks a few things when it creates the item, and "Mantle refused the item" then gives the reason, for example:

  • "the accessory is N cm across, [slot] allows N cm" or "the accessory reaches N cm from its pivot, [slot] allows N cm"
  • "the avatar stands N cm tall, the minimum is 151.1 cm" (or "the limit is 218.1 cm")
  • "a hair item may use at most 4 textures", or "the item needs about N MB of GPU memory; the limit is 24.0 MB"
  • "An item name is 1 to 64 characters." or "A description is at most 1000 characters." (neither can contain control or invisible formatting characters, though a description can have line breaks)
  • "Every item needs a thumbnail."

When Mantle names the part at fault, its name follows in brackets, for example "(description)".

Changing an item In progress

Mantle can update a published item in place, but the Cosmetics panel doesn't offer it yet. If you change the asset, slot, name, description or any other setting and choose Upload to Mantle, the panel publishes a separate new item, and the old one stays in the catalogue. If nothing changed, uploading the same asset to the same slot again doesn't make a copy: the log under Files and details says "This item was already published ([item id]); following it" and the panel follows the existing item.

When editing arrives, an edited item is checked and screened again like a new one, and it leaves the catalogue until the new version passes. Each edit counts towards the hourly item limit.

Delisting an item In progress

Delisting takes an item out of the catalogue for good. The panel can't delist yet. A delisted item can't be worn or edited, and players wearing it have it taken off, and the Avatar screen tells them (for example "1 item is no longer available and was taken off"). To publish it again, upload it as a new item.

Reports and takedowns

  • Players can report an item with Report this item at the bottom of its page on the Avatar screen. See Report an avatar item.
  • A moderator can delist any item.
  • If a moderator removes one of your uploaded files from your account (usually after a licence complaint), every item of yours that uses it is delisted, and that file can't be uploaded or used again.
  • A moderator can also add a copyright strike when they take an item down. The third strike turns off public publishing for your account. You can still publish privately.
