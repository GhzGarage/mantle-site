Avatars In progress

You upload a body Skeletal Mesh, and for a MetaHuman its face too. You don't upload any animation: Mantle animates every avatar with its own animations and players' emotes.

Supported skeletons

SkeletonWhat it's forWears 3D clothingFace
MetaHuman body (metahuman_base_skel)MetaHumans and bodies made on the MetaHuman skeletonYes, if exported the right way (see MetaHumans that wear clothing)Optional MetaHuman face
UE5 Mannequin (SK_Mannequin, the UE 5.4 and later skeleton with its corrective bones)Stylised characters and Fab characters rigged to the UE5 MannequinNoNone: the head is part of the body

The UEFN mannequin skeleton is only for emotes, not avatars.

To see which skeleton your mesh uses, open the Skeletal Mesh and look at its Skeleton asset. You don't choose it in the panel: the plugin tries both and picks the one that fits. A mesh on the full SK_Mannequin skeleton is read as a UE5 Mannequin, and Check only then shows "Done: UE5 Mannequin body: it wears accessories and emotes, 3D clothing is set aside while it is worn, and it takes no MetaHuman face." A mesh skinned only to bones the two skeletons share is read as a MetaHuman body.

Bones and names

  • Every bone that carries skin weight must be a bone of the supported skeleton, with the same parent as on that skeleton.
  • Extra bones that carry no weight, such as cloth or helper bones some exports add, are left out for you.
  • The skeleton's root bone (root) must be included, and the bones must form one hierarchy.
  • Bone names match whatever their case, so Root counts as root. Two bones whose names differ only in case are refused, because Unreal treats them as one bone.
  • Up to 8 bone influences per vertex.
  • The mesh must be bound in its reference pose. Morph targets aren't uploaded.

Publishing an avatar

  1. Prepare the body

    Keep it in its reference pose with its feet on the origin, and check it fits the limits. For a MetaHuman that should wear clothing, export it as described in MetaHumans that wear clothing.

  2. Pick Avatar

    In the Mantle Cosmetics tab, pick Avatar under "What are you publishing?". The slot is avatar.

  3. Pick the body

    Under "Asset", choose the body Skeletal Mesh. If one of its LODs is over the triangle limit, the plugin uses the first LOD that fits.

  4. Add the face (MetaHuman only)

    Under "Face and sections", choose the MetaHuman's face Skeletal Mesh. See The face.

  5. Check the section roles

    Each material slot of the body is listed with a role: skin, eyes or other. See Skin tone and eye colour.

  6. Set the jaw bone if needed

    See Mouth movement.

  7. Move sockets if needed

    Only if accessories sit in the wrong place on your body. See Accessories and sockets.

  8. Name, check and upload

    Give it a name, choose Check only, fix any "Problem" lines, then Upload to Mantle. The thumbnail shows the avatar full body with its face.

MetaHumans that wear clothing

3D clothing, hair, eyebrows, eyelashes, facial hair and 2D clothing fit an avatar only if its body is exported the right way: export the MetaHuman with UE Optimized at Medium or High quality, without clothing. The plugin finds the right LOD and uploads it, and Check only shows "Done: Garment-compatible: LOD N has Mantle's reference topology, so it takes 3D clothing."

A MetaHuman body without that LOD still uploads, with "Note: not garment-compatible: export the MetaHuman with UE Optimized at Medium or High quality, without clothing. It still uploads: it wears accessories and emotes, not 3D clothing." A UE5 Mannequin avatar never wears clothing.

Players on an avatar that can't wear clothing keep their clothing and hair: those items are marked "SET ASIDE" on the Avatar screen and go back on when they switch to an avatar that fits them. Accessories and emotes work on every avatar.

The face

  • Only a MetaHuman body takes a face, and it must be a MetaHuman face on the MetaHuman face skeleton. A UE5 Mannequin's head is part of its body.
  • A MetaHuman body ends at the neck, so its face counts towards the avatar's height. Without the face, a MetaHuman body is usually too short to pass the minimum height.
  • The plugin uploads a LOD of the face that fits the 10,000-triangle limit.
  • The face's skin takes the player's skin tone and its eyes the eye colour. MetaHuman's see-through face parts (eye shell, eye edge, eyelashes, saliva and cartilage) aren't drawn. Players add eyelashes, eyebrows and facial hair as separate items in the face slots, which are fitted like hair.

Skin tone and eye colour

When you publish, Mantle converts each section's materials from its base colour, normal and ORM textures (see Making avatar items). The panel fills in a role for each section from its material slot name; change it if it's wrong. The role decides what the player's choices change:

RoleWhat happens
skinTakes the player's Skin tone
eyesTakes the player's Eye colour on the iris
otherShown as you made it
  • The Avatar screen shows the Skin tone row only if the avatar has a skin section, and Eye colour only if it has an eyes section. With neither, players see "This avatar has its own skin and eyes: there are no colours to pick." Mark everything other if your character's colours are part of its design.
  • The "Body default" skin tone and eye colour show the avatar exactly as you exported it.
  • Eye colour looks right on eyeballs UV-mapped like a MetaHuman eye.
  • Any material slot whose name contains eyeshell, eyeedge, eyelash, saliva or cartilage and has the other role isn't drawn, so rename a slot you want to keep.

Height

Your avatar must stand between 151.1 cm and 218.1 cm tall (a MetaHuman measured with its face). Players can then change it with the Height slider, from 95% to 105%, which scales the whole avatar.

Accessories and sockets

Accessories attach to eight sockets that come with Mantle's copy of each supported skeleton, so your mesh doesn't need them. acc_hat and acc_face are on head, acc_neck on neck_01, acc_shoulder_l and acc_shoulder_r on clavicle_l and clavicle_r, acc_front on spine_04, acc_back on spine_03 and acc_waist on pelvis.

If your body's proportions put a hat, glasses or backpack in the wrong place, move that socket under Socket offsets ("Move where accessories attach (optional)"). If your mesh has a socket of the same name on the same bone, the values start from it. The ranges and messages are in The panel's fields. Accessories themselves are covered in Clothing, hair and accessories.

First person

  • With a MetaHuman face, the first-person camera sits between its eyes. Without a face, including every UE5 Mannequin avatar, it sits at a standard mannequin's eye height.
  • In first person, players don't see their own head and face, so they don't see the inside of their head. Other players still see it, and the head's shadow stays. On a body without a separate face, weight everything that would block the view to head or its child bones, so it hides too.

Mouth movement

When a player talks on voice chat, the avatar's mouth moves with their voice. Jaw bone names the bone that opens it. Left empty, it's FACIAL_C_Jaw on a MetaHuman with its face, and none otherwise, so the mouth doesn't move. Type the name of a jaw bone in your body or face to use it.

If Mantle's own processing refuses a file after the panel's checks pass, the upload stops with "Mantle's processing refused it:" and the reason. For an avatar the most likely one is that a bone's rest transform isn't the pose the mesh was bound in: export it in its reference pose.

After you publish an avatar

  • Once it passes screening, it's listed under "Avatars" in the catalogue with a badge: "WEARS CLOTHING" or "NO CLOTHING: ACCESSORIES ONLY".
  • The catalogue's "Fits my avatar" filter, which is on by default, only lists avatars that wear clothing. Players see an avatar that doesn't when they turn the filter off.
  • Players can wear it with their own skin tone, eye colour and height, and play their emotes on it.

Emotes In progress

An emote is an Anim Sequence on the UEFN mannequin skeleton (SK_UEFN_Mannequin). Mantle plays it on whichever avatar the player wears, so one emote works on MetaHuman and UE5 Mannequin avatars alike.

Publishing an emote

  1. Make the animation on the UEFN mannequin

    Animate on SK_UEFN_Mannequin, or retarget an existing animation onto it (right-click it and choose Retarget Animations) and use the retargeted copy.

  2. Keep it in place

    Keep the root bone within 50 cm of where it starts, and animate pelvis. See Emote rules.

  3. Mark a loop if you want one

    Add an Anim Notify named loop_start where the loop should begin. See Looping.

  4. Pick Emote

    In the Mantle Cosmetics tab, pick Emote under "What are you publishing?". The slot is emote. Under "Asset", choose the Anim Sequence.

  5. Name, check and upload

    Choose Check only, fix any "Problem" lines, then Upload to Mantle.

Emote rules

RuleValue
SkeletonUEFN mannequin. Every animated bone must be one of its bones (names match whatever their case).
LengthUp to 10 seconds
Frame rateAny
Required trackpelvis
Root motionThe root bone can move at most 50 cm from its first frame. Emotes play in place: the player doesn't travel with the root.
Anim NotifiesOnly footstep_l, footstep_r, impact and loop_start are kept, up to 64. Other notifies are left out.
SizeUp to 512 KiB once converted. Shorter clips that animate fewer bones are smaller.

Looping

Without a loop_start notify, the emote plays once and stops at its last frame. With one, it plays to the end, then loops from the notify's time until the player stops it. Put loop_start at 0 to loop the whole clip.

How players use emotes

  • Players get emotes from the catalogue and wear up to 8 at a time. They play them from the emote wheel: hold B or right bumper, point, and let go. See Emotes for players.
  • The emote blends in over 0.2 s and out over 0.25 s. Everyone in the world sees it at the same point in the clip, even if they join partway through.
  • It stops when the player moves, jumps or falls, sits down, gets hit, dies or ragdolls. It can't start while they're moving, in the air, seated or dead, or within half a second of their last emote.
  • World scripts can play one of a player's equipped emotes with Avatar:PlayEmote(itemId). See the API reference.
