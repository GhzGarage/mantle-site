What happens when you publish

  • Left out with a warning: BSP brushes, engine volumes (such as Trigger Volume and Blocking Volume), placed pawns, landscape and foliage. Use static meshes instead, and use Trigger Box or Trigger Sphere actors for trigger areas.
  • Left out with a warning: SkyAtmosphere, SkyLight, VolumetricCloud and ExponentialHeightFog ("Mantle provides the sky; this actor's settings are not published.").
  • Converted and uploaded: meshes and materials from your project. See Your own assets.
  • Stops the publish with an error: unsupported actors, post-process volumes, extra components, Ambient Sounds, names, tags or material overrides over the limits below, meshes over the mesh limits, and an empty level. See What "Check this place" catches.

Assets

Built-in library Live

The library has 15 items. Use them straight from these folders so Mantle recognises them:

  • Meshes: the cube, sphere, cylinder, cone and plane from /Engine/BasicShapes, and the cube, plane, ramp, quarter cylinder, chamfered cube and cylinder from /Game/LevelPrototyping.
  • Materials: a basic material and three prototype grid materials.
  • The library has no sounds or textures. The prototyping meshes don't all have their pivot in the same place.

Your own assets Live

You can use static meshes and materials from your own project, such as a Fab pack. You don't upload them yourself: when you publish, Mantle picks up every mesh and material the level uses that isn't in the library and gets it ready for the game. Later publishes are quicker, because files you've already uploaded aren't sent again. For the full walkthrough, every limit and message, and how to fix problems, see Custom meshes and materials.

MeshUp to 500,000 triangles, 1,500,000 vertices and 8 material slots. Nanite meshes are sent as their fallback mesh, so raise Fallback Triangle Percent if it looks too coarse. A mesh whose material sections share vertices can't be converted.

Sky and environment

  • Live Default sky: Mantle adds an atmosphere, a real-time sky light, clouds and fog to your world. If your place has no Directional Light, Mantle also adds a sun (intensity 3.5).
  • Live Lights are all Movable in the published world, and no lighting is baked.
  • Planned Custom sky.
  • Planned Post-process.
