Vehicles publish with the next release

The vehicle converter and the Mantle Vehicle Spawn are in the Mantle plugin, so you can build and check a car now. A place with a vehicle can be saved to Mantle, but Publish stops with "This revision is not compatible with the supported release matrix." until the game release that reads vehicles is out. The editor's Output Log says "This place uses VehicleSpawn, which the released game can't build yet." Scripts can already spawn Mantle's built-in cars with Vehicles:Spawn.

How it works Next release

Tools → Mantle → Convert vehicle turns a car's skeletal mesh into a Mantle vehicle asset. It finds the wheel bones, measures the wheels and writes <Mesh>_Vehicle next to the mesh with default tuning for you to adjust. The car drives on Mantle's own vehicle physics, with the wheels spinning, steering and moving with the suspension. You then place it with a Mantle Vehicle Spawn. Players get in with F / Y and drive it with the usual vehicle controls.

The converter reads only the skeletal mesh. It doesn't use the car's Blueprint, Chaos Vehicle movement settings, physics asset, animation blueprint or Control Rig, and static meshes attached to the Blueprint don't come across. Your original car isn't changed.

What you can convert

Any skeletal mesh that is the car's body, with a bone at the centre of each wheel named like a wheel:

  • The bone's name contains wheel (any case) and ends in its corner: _FL or _FR at the front, _ML or _MR in the middle, _BL/_BR or _RL/_RR at the back. For example Wheel_FL.
  • 2 to 8 wheels. Front wheels steer, middle and back wheels drive, and the last axle has the handbrake.
  • At most 128 bones, 200,000 triangles and 8 materials.

Converting a car

  1. Select the body

    Select the car's skeletal mesh in the Content Browser, then choose Tools → Mantle → Convert vehicle. With nothing selected, it says "Select the vehicle's skeletal mesh in the Content Browser first: its body, with a bone at each wheel centre named like Wheel_FL, Wheel_FR, Wheel_BL and Wheel_BR."

  2. Read the notes

    When it works, you'll see "[Mesh]: made [Mesh]_Vehicle (not saved yet). Place a Mantle Vehicle Spawn and set its Vehicle to it.", with notes on what it found: each wheel ("Wheel_FL steers; measured radius 34 cm, width 22 cm."), which material sections it painted, and how many suspension rules it found. Each wheel is measured from the geometry weighted to its bone alone. If it can't be measured, set the wheel's Tire Mesh, or its Radius and Width, in the vehicle asset.

  3. Tune it

    Open [Mesh]_Vehicle and adjust it (see What you get), then save it. Converting again keeps your tuning, your wheel settings, your paint sections and your suspension rules.

  4. Place it

    Drag a Mantle Vehicle Spawn into the level and set its Vehicle to the asset. A place can have 32.

What you get

The vehicle asset holds everything Mantle needs:

SectionWhat's in it
Body and WheelsThe skeletal mesh, and for each wheel its bone, whether it steers, drives or has the handbrake, its radius (up to 150 cm) and width (up to 100 cm), and an optional separate Tire Mesh with its transform (a negative scale mirrors a right-hand tyre).
TuningMass (50 to 20,000 kg), top speed, centre of mass, drag, engine torque curve (2 to 16 points), gears (1 to 8), suspension, steering angle (5 to 70°), brakes and tyres, the driver's Seat Location, the camera, and up to 8 collision boxes. Without a Seat Location, the driver sits behind the middle on the left; without collision boxes, the car collides as one box around the body.
PaintThe material sections that are paint, and the paint's colour, metallic, roughness, flakes and clear coat. Painted sections use Mantle's car paint, and scripts can recolour them with Vehicles:SetPaintColor. The converter fills in sections whose material is a Clear Coat material.
LiveriesUp to 8 images laid over the paint, with alpha where the paint shows through. They use the mesh's first UV channel. Default Livery picks the one it starts with (-1 for none), and scripts can switch them with Vehicles:SetLivery (script API 1.20).
LinkageUp to 64 rules that move suspension parts with the wheels (aim, stretch or copy), found from bone names such as hub, knuckle, damper and shock. Empty it and convert again to have Mantle find them again.

The Mantle Vehicle Spawn has Vehicle, Respawn Seconds (0 to 600, 5 by default), Enabled and Livery (-1 uses the vehicle's default).

Suspension linkage waits for its own release

A vehicle with Linkage rules can't be uploaded until a game release reads them: "…is not read by the released game yet; upload this vehicle once a Mantle release reads linkage, or remove it for now". To upload the car without them, empty Linkage in the vehicle asset.

What's different

  • Handling: the car starts with Mantle's default tuning, not the original's settings. Test drive it and adjust the vehicle asset.
  • Not carried across: the Blueprint's logic, its attached static meshes (only a separate tyre, as Tire Mesh), and the original's Control Rig and animation blueprint.

When a conversion stops

The dialog says "[Mesh]: not converted." and lists the problems:

ProblemWhat to do
[Mesh] has N wheel bones (…); a vehicle has 2 to 8.Name each wheel-centre bone with "wheel" and its corner, as in What you can convert, and re-import it.
[Mesh] has no front wheel bone to steer withName the front wheels' bones ending in _FL and _FR, and re-import it.
[Mesh]'s bones … and … are both named like wheels and sit at the same place.Rename the one that isn't the wheel's centre so it doesn't end in a corner, and re-import it.
[Mesh] has N bones; a vehicle body has at most 128Use a simpler skeleton.
[Mesh] has N triangles; a vehicle body has at most 200000.Use a lower-detail version of the body.
[Mesh] has N materials; a vehicle body has at most 8.Merge materials until it has 8 or fewer.
[path] already exists and is not a Mantle vehicle. Rename or move it, then convert again.As the message says.

"Check this place" also checks each Vehicle Spawn: "has no vehicle to park." (run Convert vehicle and set the spawn's Vehicle), a livery number the vehicle doesn't have, and more than 32 spawns.
