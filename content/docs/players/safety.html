Reporting

Report a player Live

Open a creator's profile page and choose Report. You'll see "Choose the main reason. Your report is private and requests human review." and these reasons:

  • Harassment
  • Hate speech
  • Sexual content
  • Violence or gore
  • Spam
  • Copyright / ownership
  • Something else

You can add more details if you like (up to 1,000 characters). Once it's sent, you'll see "Report received. Thank you for helping keep Mantle safe." The player you report is never told who reported them.

Report a chat message Live

Every chat message in a world has a Report button. Choose the main reason from the same list as above, or choose Cancel. Your report includes the message, so moderators can see exactly what was said. You'll see "Report sent. Thanks for keeping this place friendly."

Report a community server Live

Each community-run server listed on a world page has a Report link.

Report a world Live

Open the world's page and choose Report this world on its About tab. Then choose a reason, as when reporting a player.

Report an avatar item Live

Open an item on the Avatar screen and choose Report this item at the bottom of its page.

Report voice chat Planned

A way to report voice chat directly is coming later. For now, report the player from their profile or from one of their chat messages, or block them.

What happens to a report

  • Reports go to Mantle's moderators, who are real people. Only moderators can read them.
  • If you report the same thing again, it updates your earlier report instead of creating a new one.
  • Each account can send up to 20 reports an hour. After that, you'll see "That is more reports than one account can file in an hour."

Blocking Live

Block someone from their profile page or from one of their chat messages. You'll see "Their profile, worlds and presence will be hidden for you, and their chat muted in any world you share. They will not be notified."

  • Their worlds disappear from your Discover page and charts. Chat between you is hidden both ways, and their voice is muted for you.
  • Unblock anyone from Settings → Safety → Blocked players.
  • Mantle keeps its own block list, separate from Steam's or Epic's.

Moderation

Content screening Live

Uploaded pictures (including profile pictures), avatar items and the scripts creators write are checked automatically before anyone else can see them. If something is flagged, a moderator reviews it, and it can't be published until it's approved.

If your profile picture is still being checked, you'll see "That avatar has not passed review."

What moderators can do

  • Live Approve or reject flagged uploads.
  • Live Shut down a community server.
  • Live Automatic chat filtering. Some words are replaced with asterisks, and some messages aren't sent at all (see text chat).
  • Planned Warn, mute, kick or ban players, take down worlds, and handle appeals.
  • Planned Automatic filtering of display names.

World creators can also remove players from their own worlds.

Your account

Account and sign-in Live

Your Mantle account is created the first time you sign in. There's no separate sign-up. If you play on both Steam and Epic, both use the same Mantle account.

Display name Live

Your name comes from the store you signed in with (Steam or Epic) and updates each time you sign in. Settings says "Your name comes from [store]: change it there".

Names can be up to 32 characters.

What your profile holds

  • Your display name and profile picture.
  • Your avatar's appearance, saved outfits and the avatar items you've got.
  • Your settings: subtitles, voice mode, text chat, reduce motion, high contrast and streamer mode.
  • Your favourites, block list and published worlds.
  • Any creator tokens and server keys you've made. You'll find them in Settings → Account. Revoke any you no longer use.

Other players can see your name, picture, join date and published worlds. They can't see your Epic or Steam account IDs.

Deleting your account or data In progress

You can't delete your account from inside Mantle yet. For now, ask us to delete it as described in the privacy policy. Deleting an account can't be undone. It:

  • Removes your name and profile picture, your avatar, outfits and items, your settings, favourites and block list, and the progress worlds saved for you.
  • Takes down your published worlds and avatar items. Deleted worlds are removed for good after 180 days.
  • Revokes your creator tokens and server keys.
  • Keeps chat messages you sent, shown as "Deleted user", until they expire (see Privacy).

If you sign in again later with the same Epic or Steam account, you start a new, empty Mantle account.

Planned Signing out, deleting your account and downloading a copy of your data from inside Mantle are coming later.

Privacy

To run Mantle, we record when you join and leave worlds (along with your platform and country), store chat messages sent in worlds, and keep reports and block lists. Voice chat is off by default.

We don't keep this forever:

  • Chat messages are deleted after about 90 days.
  • Your play history (which worlds you joined, and when) is deleted after about a year.
  • Reports are deleted a year after moderators resolve them.

The privacy policy has the full details.
