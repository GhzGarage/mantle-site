What your PC needs

  • Windows, 64-bit. Mantle is only built for 64-bit Windows PCs.
  • Steam or the Epic Games Store. Mantle is in closed testing on both. Start it from Steam or the Epic Games Launcher, because that's how you sign in. See Installing.
  • An internet connection. You need one to sign in, browse worlds and play.
  • A microphone if you want to talk on voice chat. You can play without one.

Minimum PC specs haven't been published yet. If a world runs slowly, see Slow or choppy worlds below.

Common problems

Can't sign in

Mantle signs you in with the store you started it from. Most problems go away if you start Mantle from Steam or the Epic Games Launcher, then press "TRY SIGN-IN AGAIN".

If you see…What to do
Start Mantle from Steam or the Epic Games Launcher to sign in.Close Mantle and start it from your Steam or Epic library.
Steam is not running. Start Mantle from Steam and try again.Open Steam and sign in, then start Mantle from Steam.
Steam could not confirm your account. Check that Steam is online and try again.Make sure Steam isn't in offline mode and your internet is working.
Epic sign-in was cancelled or could not be completed (…). Try again when you're ready.Press "TRY SIGN-IN AGAIN" and finish signing in in Epic's window.
Epic sign-in is unavailable. Please try again. / Epic signed in, but Mantle could not verify the session. Please try again.Try again in a moment. If it keeps happening, check your connection.
Epic Online Services could not start. Check the Mantle installation and try again. / Mantle's online services could not start. Check the Mantle installation and try again.Use Steam's or the Epic Games Launcher's option to verify (check) the game files, then start Mantle again.
Signed in from another location.Your account signed in on another PC. Close Mantle there if you want to play here.

The full list of sign-in messages is in Signing in.

Can't join a world

If a join fails, the joining screen shows the reason with "BACK" and "TRY AGAIN". Pressing "PLAY" never fails because a server is full: if every server for that world is full, Mantle starts a new one for you.

If you see…What to do
This version of Mantle is out of date. Update it through Steam or the Epic Games Store, then try again.Let Steam or the Epic Games Launcher update Mantle. If no update starts, restart the store app. Then join again.
That place could not be read. It may need a newer version of Mantle.Update Mantle the same way, then try again.
Could not reach Mantle. Check your internet connection and try again.Check your connection, then press "TRY AGAIN".
Couldn't join [world]. Check your connection and try again.Check your connection, then press "TRY AGAIN".
Your server took too long to start. Try joining again.Press "TRY AGAIN". A new server sometimes needs a second attempt.
That world couldn't be loaded. Try joining again.You're taken back to the home screen. Join the world again.
Sessions are unavailable. / Something went wrong.The problem is on our side. Wait a few minutes and try again. If it doesn't clear up, let us know.
A join is already in progress.Wait for the current join to finish, or press "CANCEL" first.

All the joining messages are listed in Joining a world.

Can't join a community server

  • "Full": the server has no room, so "JOIN" is turned off. Pick another server, or press "PLAY" for an official one.
  • "That server is no longer available. Refreshing the list…": the server closed or couldn't take you. The list reloads, so pick another server. If this happens on every server, check that Mantle is up to date.
  • "Older version" or "Not yet verified": you can still join. These tags are just for your information.
  • No "Servers" tab: the world has no community servers right now. "PLAY" still works.

See Community servers for more.

Voice chat isn't working

  • Voice is off by default. Go to Settings → Chat → Voice chat and choose "Push to talk" or "Open mic". The change takes effect straight away, even in a world.
  • On "Push to talk", you're only heard while you hold V (D-pad down on a gamepad). Choose "Open mic" to talk without holding a key.
  • Voice only works in a world, and only with players close to you. Beyond 25 m, you can't hear each other. See Voice chat.
  • Can't hear one person? If you've blocked them, their voice is muted. Check Settings → Safety.
  • Microphone: Mantle doesn't have a microphone setting of its own. Check that your microphone works in Windows, is set as your default input, and isn't muted.

Text chat isn't showing, or messages don't send

  • Chat doesn't open: check Settings → Chat → Text chat. When it's off, chat never appears and T does nothing. Chat only works while you're in a world.
  • "You are sending messages too quickly. Wait a moment and try again.": you can send up to 30 messages a minute. Wait a little, then send again.
  • "That message was not sent because it breaks the chat rules.": the chat filter stopped it. Reword it.
  • "Messages can be up to 500 characters.": shorten your message.
  • "Chat can't reach Mantle right now. Retrying." or "That message didn't send. Try again.": a connection problem. Wait a moment and try again.
  • Someone's messages are missing: you won't see messages from players you've blocked, or who have blocked you.

More in Text chat.

Avatar or items missing

  • Your look didn't stick: changes on the Avatar screen only keep once you press "Save". While "Unsaved changes" shows, they haven't been saved.
  • An item was taken off: if something you wore is no longer available, Mantle takes it off and tells you once. For example: "Your avatar is no longer available, so you're back to the base avatar."
  • Clothing or hair marked "SET ASIDE": it doesn't fit your current avatar. It's still yours and goes back on when you switch to an avatar it fits.
  • "Couldn't load the catalog.", "Couldn't load your items." or "Couldn't load your outfits.": press "Retry". If it keeps failing, check your connection.
  • "Couldn't save that outfit. Try again.": try again in a moment. You can save up to 50 outfits.

See Avatar for how the Avatar screen works.

Slow or choppy worlds

Open Settings (the gear in the top bar, or Esc in a world) and try these:

  1. Auto-detect quality

    In the Graphics tab, press "Detect". Mantle measures your PC and picks settings to match. It applies straight away.

  2. Lower the preset

    Set "Graphics quality" to "Balanced" or "Low".

  3. Lower the 3D resolution scale

    This draws the world at a lower resolution without resizing the window.

  4. See what's happening

    In the Display tab, set "Performance stats" to "FPS", or to "Full" to also see your ping and packet loss in a world. High ping or packet loss points to your connection rather than your PC.

All the options are listed in Menu and settings.

Getting help

If something still isn't working, email contact@mantleplatform.com or ask in the Mantle Discord.

When you report a problem, tell us:

  • what you were doing when it happened
  • the world you were in or trying to join
  • the exact message you saw, word for word (a screenshot helps)
  • whether you started Mantle from Steam or Epic

To report a player, a chat message, a world, a community server or an avatar item, use Report in Mantle instead. See Reporting.
