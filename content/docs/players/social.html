Friends and presence Live

Mantle uses your existing friends from Epic (or from Steam, if you started Mantle from Steam). It only shows friends who have a Mantle account, and it hides anyone you've blocked. To add or remove friends, use the Epic or Steam overlay.

  • Friends in the top bar: you'll see up to three faces and "[N] online", which counts friends who are in Mantle right now. Click it to open the Steam or Epic overlay.
  • "Friends are playing": a row on the Discover page showing the worlds your friends are in. A world's page shows "[Name] is here" or "A, B and C are here".
  • Your status: "Playing [world]", "Playing on Mantle" or "Browsing Mantle".
  • Joining a friend: use the Join button in the Epic or Steam overlay. If you press it while you're in a world, you'll join once you're back at the home screen.

Planned Invites, parties, lobbies and a Join button inside Mantle are coming later. For now, join friends through the Steam or Epic overlay.

Text chat Live

Press T (View on a gamepad) to open chat in a world. To close it, press Enter on an empty line, or press Esc.

Chat is on by default. To turn it off, go to Settings → Chat → Text chat ("Off, it never appears and the key does nothing").

  • One chat per server. Everyone in the world with you shares the same chat, and you need to be in that world to use it. There are no team, party or private-message chats.
  • Limits: 500 characters per message, and 30 messages per minute (across all worlds).
  • When you open chat, you'll see the last 50 messages.
  • Chat is filtered before anyone sees it. Some words are replaced with asterisks, and some messages aren't sent at all. A message that isn't sent still counts towards your 30 a minute.
  • Each message has Block and Report buttons. Report asks you to choose a reason (see reporting chat).

Voice chat Live

Everyone in the world with you shares one voice chat. Choose how it works in Settings → Chat → Voice chat:

  • "Off" (the default)
  • "Push to talk" (hold V or D-pad down)
  • "Open mic"

Good to know:

  • Distance matters: you hear nearby players at full volume. Further away they get quieter, and beyond 25 m you can't hear them at all. Distance limits who can hear you, but don't treat voice chat as private.
  • Talking avatars: a player's avatar moves its mouth while they speak.
  • To silence someone, block them. This mutes their voice for you.
  • Planned Age and permission controls for voice chat are coming later (see Age and parental controls).

Blocking Live

You can block someone in two ways:

  • From a chat message: you'll see "You blocked [Name]. Their voice is muted too." Their earlier messages disappear from your chat as well.
  • From the menu on a creator's page: choose "Block [Name]?", then "BLOCK". You'll see "Blocked. Their content is now hidden."

The player you block is never told. Blocking someone:

  • Hides chat between you, in both directions, from then on.
  • Mutes their voice.
  • Hides their profile, removes them from your friend list in Mantle, and hides their worlds from your Discover page and favourites.
  • Mantle has its own block list. Blocking someone on Epic doesn't block them in Mantle.

Manage your blocks in Settings → Safety. You'll see "BLOCKED PLAYERS · N", with "UNBLOCK" next to each player. If you haven't blocked anyone, it says "You haven't blocked anyone. Block from a creator's page, or from a chat line in a world."

Profiles and display names Live

  • Your name comes from Epic or Steam, and you can't change it in Mantle. (Settings → Account shows "Your [store] name".)
  • Profile picture: "PNG or JPEG, up to 2048 by 2048 and 8 MB". It's checked before anyone else can see it.
  • Your public profile shows your name, picture, join date and how many worlds you've published.

Planned Following, groups and social profiles are coming later.

Discord Live

If the Discord app is running, your Discord status shows "In a world" or "Browsing". While you're in a world, it also shows "Playing [world]" and how long you've been playing.

For now, Discord only shows what you're doing. You can't link your Discord account or join worlds from Discord.
