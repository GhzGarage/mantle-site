Movement and camera Live

  • Slide: crouch while sprinting.
  • Climbing over things: jump at an obstacle and you'll vault, hurdle, mantle or climb it automatically. You vault, hurdle or mantle obstacles up to 125 cm tall, and climb ledges up to 275 cm.
  • Camera: choose first person, over-the-shoulder or third person. When you aim, the view zooms in. In first person, the camera sits at your avatar's eyes and you don't see your own face.

Avatar In progress

Press "AVATAR" in the top bar to open the Avatar screen. Mantle's menu inside a world doesn't have it, but a world's creator can add a button that opens it. Your character shows on the left, and the right side has three tabs: "Catalog", "Owned" and "Outfits".

  • Catalog: browse by "All", "Avatars", "Clothing", "Hair", "Accessories" or "Emotes", then narrow Clothing, Hair and Accessories further (for example "Tops", "Shoes", "Facial hair" or "Hats"). Use "Search the catalog" to search by an item's name or its creator. Sort by "Newest" or "Oldest", and press "Show more" for the next page. Everything listed is free.
  • Catalog filters: "Fits my avatar" is on by default, so you only see items your current avatar can wear. "By Mantle" shows only Mantle's own items. Press "More by [creator]" on an item to see just that creator's items. The filter then shows as "By [creator]", and pressing it clears it.
  • Owned: everything you've got, sorted by "Recently added" or "Name". Owned also has a "Body" section with "Skin tone", "Eye colour" and "Height" (95% to 105%, "Shorter" to "Taller"). Some avatars have their own skin and eyes, so there are no colours to pick.
  • Outfits: "Save current look" saves what you're wearing as an outfit (up to 50). Each outfit has "Update to this look", "Rename" and "Delete". An outfit name can be 1 to 40 characters.
  • Buttons: "Try on", "Revert", "Get and wear" (or "Wear" if you own it), "Take off" and "Report this item". Frame the preview with "Full", "Head", "Body" or "Feet".
  • Saving: changes show on the preview straight away, with "Undo" and "Redo". Press "Save" to keep them. Until then you'll see "Unsaved changes". Your saved look follows you into every world, including your avatar, clothing, hair, accessories, skin tone, eye colour and height.
  • Limits: one item per slot, up to 8 accessories and up to 8 emotes.
  • Set aside: some avatars can't wear clothing. If you switch to one, clothing and hair that don't fit are marked "SET ASIDE". They're still yours and go back on when you switch to an avatar they fit.
  • Items that are gone: if something in your saved look is no longer available, it's taken off and the Avatar screen tells you once, with "Dismiss". For example: "Your avatar is no longer available, so you're back to the base avatar."
  • When you talk on voice chat, your avatar's mouth moves with your voice.

Emotes In progress

Get emotes from the catalogue and wear them from the Avatar screen, up to 8 at a time. In a world:

  1. Hold B or right bumper

    The emote wheel opens. It only opens when you're holding nothing (Fists), on foot, alive and not seated. The camera holds still while it's open.

  2. Point at an emote

    Use the mouse or the right stick.

  3. Let go

    You play the emote you're pointing at. Let go in the centre to cancel.

Everyone in the world sees the emote in sync. It stops when you move, jump or fall, sit down, get hit or die. You can't start one while moving, in the air, seated or dead.

Tools, backpack and weapons Live

These only appear in worlds that give out tools (see the note at the top).

  • Picking up: walk to a pickup and press E ("Pick up [tool]"). By default, a pickup comes back after 10 s.
  • Backpack: 10 hotbar slots and room for up to 32 tools. Press the slot of the tool you're holding to put it away and go back to Fists.
  • Dropping: press G to drop your tool in front of you. It becomes a new pickup, with its ammo refilled.
  • Dying: tools you got from pickups can drop when you die, if that pickup allows it.
  • On screen: health (100 max), an ammo counter, "RELOADING", and the hotbar with counts and cooldowns.

Vehicles Live

Vehicles also only appear in worlds where the creator has added them. Walk within 4.5 m of an empty car and press F / Y ("Drive"). For now, only the driver can ride.

  • Accelerate: W / right trigger
  • Brake: S / left trigger (hold it when stopped to reverse)
  • Handbrake: Space / A
  • Steer: A/D / left stick
  • Get out: F / Y
  • Change paint colour: C / X cycles through six colours.
  • Camera: first person, close chase or far chase. It moves back behind the car shortly after you stop looking around.
  • The screen shows your speed in KM/H and the current gear.

Menu and settings Live

Esc opens the settings menu on top of the world. The game doesn't pause. While the menu is open, your cursor appears and your character won't respond to controls.

  • The menu has "Leave world" and "Quit game".
  • Each tab is marked "Follows you" (saved to your account, so it applies wherever you play) or "On this PC" (saved on this computer only).

Joining, leaving and respawning

  • Live Leave world takes you back to the home screen.
  • Live Respawning happens automatically: 5 s after you die, you reappear at a spawn point. By default it's the one nearest to where you died, but a world can choose a random one or always the first. A death screen and countdown are coming later.
  • What's saved: your avatar, outfits and "Follows you" settings save to your account. "On this PC" settings save on your computer. Your backpack and ammo reset each time you join a world.
