Mantle / Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Mantle is published and operated by Joshua Eger. This notice explains how Joshua Eger handles information when you use the Mantle website or sign in to Mantle with an Epic Games or Steam account.

Information we receive

The website does not offer accounts or forms. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include so we can respond.

When you choose to sign in to Mantle with Epic or Steam, we may receive the information made available by the permissions you approve: with Epic, your Epic account ID, display name, language, country, and linked-account display names; with Steam, your Steam ID and Steam display name. To provide social features, Mantle may also process your Epic friends list, blocked-player list, and online presence. Mantle does not receive or store your Epic Games password; you enter it only in Epic’s sign-in flow.

Depending on the features you use, Mantle may process profile information, worlds and content you publish, multiplayer and voice-session information, reports and moderation records, and basic technical information needed to run, secure, and troubleshoot the service. This includes earlier saved values of game data (DataStore), activity summaries of the web requests that server scripts make, and the Secrets that creators store for their places.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate you, operate multiplayer and social features, display your profile and presence where you choose to use those features, provide support, enforce safety rules, investigate abuse, and maintain the security and reliability of Mantle.

How information is shared

We share information only as needed to run Mantle: with Epic Games and Epic Online Services for Epic account and online-service functionality, with Valve for Steam sign-in, with service providers that help operate the application, with the operator of a community server you join (see below), when you direct us to share it through a multiplayer or social feature, or when required by law. We do not sell personal information.

Our service providers include OpenAI, which checks uploaded content and chat text for safety (moderation); Cloudflare, which hosts Mantle’s own edge service, where published files are stored and delivered; and Mantle’s voice service, a Cloudflare Worker that Mantle runs. The voice service checks your Epic sign-in and asks Epic Online Services for a short-lived token that lets you join a voice room. Voice audio itself is carried live by Epic Online Services; Mantle does not record it.

Some sessions run on community servers operated by other people, not by us. When you join one, its operator’s server receives your IP address, Mantle user ID, display name and the chat you send. Operators must follow the Community Server Operator Terms, which limit how they may use this information.

Children under 13

Mantle is a general-audience service and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without a parent’s consent. If we learn that we have, we will delete that account and its information. Parents can contact us at the address below.

Retention and choices

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate Mantle, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. You can manage Epic account permissions in your Epic account settings. To ask about access, correction, deletion, or other privacy requests, contact us at the address below.

When an account is deleted, the account’s data is purged after 30 days, except for what must be kept to keep our records consistent.

Game data that a script saves for a player can be restored by Mantle staff after a mistake, so we keep earlier saved values of that data for about 30 days. You receive the earlier values saved for your account in a data export, and they are deleted if your account is deleted.

When a server script makes a web request, the game server sends Mantle a once-a-minute summary of the web requests it made, so abuse reports can be traced after the server’s own logs are gone. The summary lists the website host, the script, request counts, and amounts of data sent and received. It does not include the full web address, query strings, headers, or the content of requests and responses. We keep these summaries for about 180 days. A community server operator can turn script web requests off, in which case no summary is sent.

Creators can store Secrets, such as API keys, for their places. Secret values are stored encrypted and are kept until the creator deletes them or deletes the place or their account. Values are never included in a data export; the export lists only the names of your Secrets and when they were changed.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email contact@mantleplatform.com.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as Mantle develops. We will post the revised version here and update the date above.
