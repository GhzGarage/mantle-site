Mantle / Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Mantle is published and operated by QBCore Studios. This notice explains how QBCore Studios handles information when you use the Mantle website or sign in to Mantle with an Epic Games account.

Information we receive

The website does not offer accounts or forms. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include so we can respond.

When you choose to sign in to Mantle with Epic, we may receive the information made available by the permissions you approve: your Epic account ID, display name, language, country, and linked-account display names. To provide social features, Mantle may also process your Epic friends list, blocked-player list, and online presence. Mantle does not receive or store your Epic Games password; you enter it only in Epic’s sign-in flow.

Depending on the features you use, Mantle may process profile information, worlds and content you publish, multiplayer and voice-session information, reports and moderation records, and basic technical information needed to run, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate you, operate multiplayer and social features, display your profile and presence where you choose to use those features, provide support, enforce safety rules, investigate abuse, and maintain the security and reliability of Mantle.

How information is shared

We share information only as needed to run Mantle: with Epic Games and Epic Online Services for Epic account and online-service functionality, with service providers that help operate the application, when you direct us to share it through a multiplayer or social feature, or when required by law. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and choices

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate Mantle, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. You can manage Epic account permissions in your Epic account settings. To ask about access, correction, deletion, or other privacy requests, contact us at the address below.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email contact@mantleplatform.com.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as Mantle develops. We will post the revised version here and update the date above.
